Most notes don't make it.

A wall of ideas is easy, and AI makes it endless. Picking the one worth building, and building it properly, is the job.

  1. Map Talk to the people who'll use it. AI turns the interviews into clear patterns by the afternoon.
  2. Cut Choose the one problem that matters and let the rest go. No tool makes that call for you.
  3. Ship Design it as a system, prototype it in real code, test it with real users and automate whatever repeats.

Five roles, then the whole product.

Seven years from junior designer to an AI-first head of product, and the one thing each role taught me.

  1. 01

    2019 · Rillusion

    Junior UI/UX Designer

    Taught me: craft first. Spacing, type and states, in fintech products where a confusing screen costs someone money.

  2. 02

    2020 · Sysvine Technologies

    UI/UX Designer

    Taught me: design the system, not the screen. Healthcare and insurance live in their edge cases.

  3. 03

    Sysvine Technologies

    Senior Product Designer

    Taught me: own the problem, not only the pixels. Research, flows and the conversation with engineering.

  4. 04

    Sysvine Technologies · to 2025

    Lead Product Designer

    Taught me: my output became the team's output. Reviews, the design system, the bar for done, and the first AI tools in our workflow.

  5. 05

    2025 – now · myAIcademy

    Head of Product Design

    Taught me: AI is a multiplier, not a shortcut. I run brand, video, writing, product, decks and automation with it, and own the roadmap, the team and the lessons that teach it.
