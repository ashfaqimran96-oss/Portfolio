MF Street

Trading, stocks and mutual funds in one app, each clearly its own.

At Rillusion, working with my mentor, I designed MF Street: one app where people trade intraday, invest in stocks and invest in mutual funds. The problem was that every kind of investment looked the same.

Every investment looked the same.

MF Street let people trade intraday, buy stocks and invest in mutual funds from one app. The types sat side by side, with nothing to tell them apart.

For an investor that is a real risk: a same-day trade and a long-term SIP carry very different stakes, and one screen for both invites the wrong choice.
