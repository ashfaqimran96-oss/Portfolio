Deciding what to build was half the work.

Every week new AI tools launch and old ones change. Someone has to decide which lessons to build, where each belongs, and then make it: screenshots, illustrations and narration.

I automated that: the pipeline runs scans, says which lesson to build and whether it is a weekly course, the featured course or a persona-based course, then assembles it.
