Lab Fellows

Lab inventory that reorders itself.

Lab Fellows supplies lab equipment and chemicals to research companies. Its product watches the inventory with AI and, when stock runs low, sends a refill request to the supplier of that exact item. At Sysvine I owned the UX thinking and the UI.

Overview

Running out of one chemical can stop a lab's work.

Challenge

Research companies need the right chemicals and equipment in stock, and noticing what is running low is slow manual work.

Approach

AI checks the inventory and, wherever stock is low, sends a refill request to the person or company that supplies that item. I designed how people see and trust that.

The shelf runs out before anyone notices.

Lab Fellows supplies lab equipment and chemicals to research companies, so the problem is inventory: knowing what is there and what is about to run out.

The product uses AI to check stock and, wherever it is low, to start a refill request to the particular supplier of that chemical or piece of equipment.
