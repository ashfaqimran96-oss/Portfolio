Several internal jobs, several owners, one platform.

Hoffmann Group is an industrial tools company. Running it takes several internal products: one for discounts on products, one for managing the products, one for how products are kept in inventory.

Each app had its own product owner and its own problem, and all of them had to live in one platform.
