HealthPractice

Patient care and doctor care, managed from A to Z.

HealthPractice is Sysvine's own product for managing a practice from A to Z, for patients and for doctors. It was the main product I worked on at Sysvine, and I was its designer.

Overview

Built from zero into the platform a clinic opens every morning.

Challenge

Manage everything a practice does, for the patient and for the doctor, in one product.

Approach

One core for the whole practice, with each part of patient and doctor care designed as part of the same system.

Outcome

More than 13,400 practitioners in 8,500+ practices use it, as its site states.

A clinic runs on a diary and a phone.

Doctors booked by phone, kept notes on paper and chased payments by hand.

Every practice wanted its own fields, so every build was bespoke and slow.

One core with modules per specialty could serve thousands of practices at once.
